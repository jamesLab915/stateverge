Disclaimer for FRB U.S. Model and the Estimated Dynamic Optimization (EDO) Model

The Board is providing the Models, including the numerical data and formulas contained in the Models, solely for the purpose of illustrating the modelling framework for projecting possible outcomes under different scenarios. Disclaimer for Interactive Data Visualizations 

This disclaimer applies to the Interactive Data Visualizations available for the Financial Accounts of the United States – Z.1 and Enhanced Financial Accounts (EFA) – as well as the Survey of Consumer Finances (SCF) and the Survey of Household Economics and Decisionmaking (SHED), which are the official releases. 

The Interactive Data Visualizations are provided solely to assist Users with a visual representation of the data and do not modify or otherwise change the official releases. Content within the Interactive Data Visualizations will be updated only after the publication of the Financial Accounts of the United States – Z.1 and EFA releases, the SCF, and the SHED. Any corrections to data will be published to the official release before it is reflected in the corresponding Interactive Data Visualizations. Should any discrepancy exist between the data contained in the official release and the Interactive Data Visualizations, Users should consult the official release for the correct data. 

To access Interactive Data Visualizations, Users must have JavaScript enabled within their browsers. 

Disclaimer for Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) and Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) APIs 

BEA: These data are BEA data. Data were accessed and retrieved using the BEA API. This product uses the Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) Data API but is not endorsed or certified by BEA. 

BLS: These data are BLS data. Data were accessed and retrieved using the BLS API. BLS.gov cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from BLS.gov. 

Data are updated generally at 8:30 a.m. on the day of release. However, there may be a lag between when updated BEA or BLS data are released, and when the data are updated on the Board's website.
