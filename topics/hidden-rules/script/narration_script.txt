### Chapter: The System  (target ~180s)
In a world intricately woven together, the global structure functions like a vast, unseen web. This web is not merely a collection of nations and economies; it is a network of relationships, influenced by power dynamics and historical contexts. In this chapter, we explore how these connections shape our societies and economies, weaving a narrative that underscores the significance of geopolitical interactions. From trade agreements to diplomatic alliances, every thread in this fabric contributes to the larger system. Understanding this global architecture is crucial to grasping the hidden rules that govern our interactions and aspirations.

### Chapter: Rupture  (target ~180s)
Yet, within this complex system, cracks begin to form. Crisis points emerge, revealing the volatility inherent in our interconnected world. Economic downturns, political upheavals, and social unrest create ripples that can destabilize not just a region, but the entirety of the global framework. In this chapter, we delve into these rupture points, examining how they challenge the stability of our networks. As we navigate through various crises, we uncover the underlying tensions that threaten to disrupt the delicate balance we often take for granted. It is here, in the face of volatility, that the hidden rules reveal their true nature, forcing us to confront the fragility of our global system.
